**Players no longer jump or get shoved across the pitch.** In Modern mode's match highlights, defenders could jump sideways out of a pass's way, slide to the ball faster than anyone runs, or be pushed along by the player on the ball, in about a third of clips. Everyone now moves at no more than a sprint: a defender who can't get there in time throws themselves at it, and a keeper who has to go further dives (#226).
