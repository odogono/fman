**Under Enhanced rules, you can play a player out of position.** Each player has a natural position: goalkeeper, defence, midfield or attack. Pick him somewhere else and he counts for less there: one skill less for each line away, and only 1 in goal unless he's a goalkeeper, or outfield if he is. With nobody in goal, your defence loses 3. On the Spectrum screen, type his number and a letter, such as `14A` to play number 14 in attack. In Modern mode, drag him to a line, or tap him and then a line. Games you've already started keep their rules (#121).
