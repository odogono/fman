**Skill shows in how a chance is finished.** In Modern mode's match highlights, a skilled striker now places their goals nearer the post and strikes them harder, and misses narrowly; a weaker one scrapes it in nearer the keeper and misses by further. A skilled keeper holds more of their saves and reaches further across. What happens in the match is unchanged (#235).
