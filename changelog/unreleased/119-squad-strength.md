**Opponents as strong as their squads in Enhanced games.** An opponent's defence, midfield and attack now come mostly from its players' skill, so a club with a strong squad plays strongly every week, and less is left to chance on the day. Clubs with stronger squads also do better in their other matches. **Clubs**, in the game menu, now shows every player's skill and each club's defence, midfield and attack, so you can see who's coming. A player you sell joins his new club's squad and makes it stronger. Games you've already started keep their rules (#119).
