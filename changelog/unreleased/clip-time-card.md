**Each clip in Modern mode's highlights now opens with a match-time card.** A pixel-art card slides in at the top left with the minute in large figures and, under it, the restart: a free kick, a corner, a throw-in or a goal kick. It replaces the small minute caption, and the kick-off and full-time stills use the same card.
