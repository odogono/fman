**A first look at Modern mode.** Switch Presentation to Modern in the game menu's Options, and the main menu becomes a Home screen: your club at a glance (division, league position, cash, loan, morale and level), your next fixture, and a big Play next match button above the menu's choices. Everything is a tap, so there is nothing to type except names. On a wide screen the Home screen stays on the left beside whatever you're doing. Screens not yet redesigned show the original screen with touch buttons. Classic mode is still the default (#43).
