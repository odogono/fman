**Under Enhanced rules, matches are told as a ticker of goals, saves and near misses.** In place of the original's highlights on the pitch, each moment of a match appears one at a time, with the minute and the player who shot. Your score counts up as the goals go in. Press any key to move on a moment, hold one to run through them, or press Skip (S) to see them all at once. Results come out about as often as they did with the original highlights. Original rules still show the pitch, and games already started keep their rules (#109).
