**Modern mode takes loans and repayments on its own screen.** You see your cash and loan, then set the amount with big − and + buttons, the +£10k, +£50k, +£100k and Max chips, or a keypad for an exact figure. The screen says the most you can borrow or repay. Take loan and Pay off loan stay greyed out until the amount is above £0, and Cancel leaves without borrowing anything. Afterwards your new cash and loan are shown before you return to the menu (#134).
