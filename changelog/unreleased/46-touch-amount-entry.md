**Loans, repayments and bids can be entered by tapping.** Amounts no longer need the phone's keyboard. Use − and + to change the amount by £1,000, or the chips to add £10k, £50k or £100k. Max jumps to the largest amount the game accepts, and Exact amount opens an on-screen keypad. Amounts never go past what the game accepts. A bid starts at the player's value (#46).
