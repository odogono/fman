**Every club plays its home matches at a ground of its own.** In Modern mode's highlights, each club's ground has its own mowing pattern and its own arrangement of stands and roofs, and its seats are in the club's colours. A club that began the game in the First Division has tall stands all round; a Fourth Division club has low terraces, sometimes with a side left bare. A club keeps its ground all game, wherever it moves in the league, and gets the same one in every new game (#273).
