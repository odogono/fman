**Promotion is within reach again at the higher levels in Enhanced games.** Since clubs got form and squads of their own, the clubs at the top of each division pulled further ahead on points, so winning promotion took far more than it used to, from the Average level upwards: at Genius most careers never left Division 4. Other clubs' results now lean less on their form and squad, and Genius is a touch easier, so promotion takes about as many points as it did before. Opponents on a hot streak are still tougher to face. Games you've already started keep their rules (#218).
