**New Enhanced games start with today's clubs and players.** The four divisions are the 2026/27 Premier League, Championship, League One and League Two, and your squad is drawn from the England squad of November 2026. Every club plays in its own colours: its name shows in them on the management screens, and its players wear its home kit in the highlights, changing kit when the two sides would clash. So there's no longer a black or white kit to choose. Original rules keep the 1982 clubs and players, and games already started keep theirs (#91).
