**Modern mode's results and league table.** After each league match, the week's results show every match in your division, yours first. The league table follows, with goal difference, arrows for each club's rise or fall since last week, your club's row picked out, and the promotion and relegation places marked. At the season end, the final table labels who is promoted, relegated or champions. Classic mode keeps the original screens.
