**Winning the F.A. Cup in Modern mode now celebrates on the pitch.** Your players run in and gather round the cup while the losers sink to the turf. Then the captain lifts it high as the crowd stands, and confetti rains down in your colours, all in time with the original's fanfare. It replaces the Spectrum-style trophy in Modern mode; Classic mode keeps the original.
