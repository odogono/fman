**In Modern-mode highlights, a wipe in both clubs' colours carries you from one clip to the next.** Thin diagonal stripes in the home kit, white and the away kit sweep across the pitch, with the next clip showing behind them, in place of a straight cut. The wipe also runs from the kick-off picture into the first clip and from the last clip into the full-time picture. Tapping or pressing Skip still plays it; tap again mid-wipe to jump straight to the next clip.
