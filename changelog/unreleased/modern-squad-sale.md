**Modern mode's squad and sale offers.** Selling a player now starts from your squad laid out as on Pick team: tap a player to put him up for sale. The offer shows him, the buying club and its offer against his value, with Accept and Refuse, and warns that turning an offer down may unsettle him, which in the original can cost him a match without telling you.
