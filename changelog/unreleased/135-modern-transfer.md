**Modern mode makes transfer bids on its own screen.** When a player is offered after a match, you see his position, number, skill, energy and value, with your cash and loan. Set your bid with the same buttons, chips and keypad as a loan, from his value up to your cash, then Place bid, or Pass to let him go. Messages such as a refused bid, a player joining or being sold, or a full squad now appear on a plain Modern screen with Continue (#135).
