**Fewer players shuffle on the spot.** In Modern mode's match highlights, a defender who ran onto a loose ball after a tackle could drift off it and lurch back onto it every few frames. An attacker could also shuffle against a defender they were waiting near: when nudged just over the offside line, or when their spot was just beyond the defender. These now hold steady. About two thirds of the shuffles left are gone.
