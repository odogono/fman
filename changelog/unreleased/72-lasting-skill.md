**Under Enhanced rules, your players keep their skill between seasons.** Every player's skill used to be rerolled at the end of the season, so a squad you bought was gone a season later. Now each player you own moves at most one point up or down, and players you don't own are still rerolled, so the transfer market stays fresh. A strong squad is worth building, though it costs more in wages for as long as you keep it. To keep the higher levels a challenge, opponents are stronger from level 2 up, so a club run on borrowed money is far more likely to be sacked there. Original rules are unchanged, and games already started keep their rules (#72, #90).
