**Penalties in Enhanced matches.** Either side can now win a penalty for a foul in the box, in about one match in four. A penalty is scored as often as any other chance, and it counts in the result. When one of your players is injured by a tackle, the penalty can be for that foul: someone else steps up to take it. The ticker marks penalties (`PEN GOAL!`), the full-time screen marks penalty goals `(pen)`, and in Modern mode the foul and the penalty play as clips. Games you've already started keep their rules (#211).
