**Defenders in Modern mode's highlights now slide in to tackle the shooter.** About a quarter of clearances are a sliding tackle as the shooter shapes to shoot: the ball runs loose, and the shooter goes down and gets back up. Some blocked shots are made sliding in, too. Keepers and tackled players now get up on hands and knees, then on one knee, rather than springing straight up.
