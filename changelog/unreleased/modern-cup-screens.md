**Modern mode's cup screens.** After a cup round you now see whether you're through to the next round (named, up to the Final) or out of the F.A. Cup, with the score of the tie. A drawn round says it goes to a replay and shows the replay's fixture at the other ground. Winning the Final plays a new celebration: the trophy builds up, the title flashes, the stars fill in, all in time with the original's beeps. Classic mode keeps the original screens.
