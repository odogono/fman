**Injuries now happen during Enhanced matches.** A player you pick can be injured in the match itself, instead of before you choose your team. It shows as a moment in the ticker, naming who made the tackle, and in Modern mode the clip shows the tackle, or the player pulling up, and the player staying down while the ball goes out. They're missing from the rest of that match's highlights, and out for the next one to three matches. Reserves aren't playing, so they're no longer injured on the bench. Tired players are still likelier to be hurt. Games you've already started keep their rules (#110).
