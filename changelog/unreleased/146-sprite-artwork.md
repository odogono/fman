**Modern mode's highlights show real players instead of coloured
circles.** Each player is a little pixel-art footballer in their kit,
with their own skin and hair, facing the way they move: running,
passing and shooting, diving, holding the ball, holding their head after
a miss and celebrating. Late in a match, tired players show a sweat drop
over their heads as the camera zooms in (#146).
