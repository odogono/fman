**The F.A. Cup final is played at a grand ground of its own.** In Modern mode's highlights, the final's clips and the cup celebration are no longer at the home side's ground but at the final ground, owned by no club: tall, roofed, seated stands on all four sides, a pitch mown in checks and claret seats and steps. The crowd at the final is split evenly between the two sides, and cheers the winners in the celebration (#274).
