**More kinds of cross in the match highlights.** In Modern mode, a ball in from wide is no longer always headed at goal. About a third of chances now come from one: a low cross or a cut-back from the byline met first time, a cross headed clear by a defender, one half-cleared and struck from the edge of the box, or the keeper coming to punch it away (#224).
