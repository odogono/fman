**Form for other clubs in Enhanced games.** Clubs in your division now go on hot streaks and slumps: each one's last three league results make it stronger or weaker as your opponent, in the league and in the cup, and change how its other matches go. In Modern mode the league table shows every club's latest results, so you can see who's on a run before you pick your team. Form starts again each season. Games you've already started keep their rules (#99).
