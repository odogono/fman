**The game can be played by tapping.** On phones and tablets, a bar of large buttons under the screen offers the answers the current prompt accepts. The Spectrum screen scales to fill the width. A finger held on the screen during a pause works like a held key. Names and amounts still open the phone's keyboard from a "Type" button. To show or hide the bar, use Touch controls under Options (#42).
