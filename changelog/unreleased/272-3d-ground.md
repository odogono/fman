**Modern mode's highlights are played on a 3D ground.** The camera looks down the pitch from behind one goal, so the stands, the goals and the advertising boards stand up off the grass and cast shadows, and zooming in no longer makes them jump in size. The players, the ball and the crowd keep their pixel art. Where the browser can't draw in 3D, the highlights show the ticker instead, as they do when the browser asks for less motion (#272).
