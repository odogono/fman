## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-11

### Added

- **Breaking.** **Original prompt beeps and keyboard clicks now play alongside measured match and cup sounds.** Audio stays in sequence through delayed frames and shortened pauses, and stops when opening the menu, muting or replacing a game. Active older session saves cannot resume under the revised timing; idle menu checkpoints still import (#27).
- **The game can be played by tapping.** On phones and tablets, a bar of large buttons under the screen offers the answers the current prompt accepts. The Spectrum screen scales to fill the width. A finger held on the screen during a pause works like a held key. Names and amounts still open the phone's keyboard from a "Type" button. To show or hide the bar, use Touch controls under Options (#42).
- **Loans, repayments and bids can be entered by tapping.** Amounts no longer need the phone's keyboard. Use − and + to change the amount by £1,000, or the chips to add £10k, £50k or £100k. Max jumps to the largest amount the game accepts, and Exact amount opens an on-screen keypad. Amounts never go past what the game accepts. A bid starts at the player's value (#46).
- **The game menu and save slots are easy to use by touch.** A menu button now sits at the top left, beside a title naming the current screen. During match highlights, the title shows who is playing. Every menu button, slot and option is large enough to tap. The menu also has Print screen, which prints the screen where the game allows it (#47).
- **The game installs as an app and plays offline.** Add it to your home screen from the browser's menu, and it opens full screen with its own icon. Once it has loaded once, it starts without a network connection. A new version arrives the next time you open it (#48).
- **Compete for the F.A. Cup.** Play every round and replay through the final,
  with original semifinal/final receipts and a trophy celebration for winning.
  Season changes remain upcoming.
- **Face the original debt-cap dismissal.** See the bills and sacking banner,
  then acknowledge the original game-clearing behaviour. A separate browser
  control lets you load a fresh game afterwards.
- **Play your first F.A. Cup tie.** Continue from the opening league match into
  the cup, with original player recovery, fatigue and injuries. Drawn ties
  replay at the opposite ground; weekly bills follow the deciding result.
- **Play your first league match.** Review team strengths, swap picked players
  and watch the original sprite highlights, then continue through the score,
  other results, league table and weekly bills. Further fixtures and the
  transfer market will follow.
- **Start a new game and manage your club.** Choose your manager, club,
  difficulty and kit colours, then inspect or sell players and manage loans
  through the original-style keyboard prompts. Match play, name editing and
  saving will follow.
- **Continue your league campaign.** Play home and away fixtures after the first
  cup tie, with cumulative results, original table ordering and gate receipts.
  After cup elimination, continue through all fifteen league matches; later cup
  rounds and season changes remain upcoming.
- **Edit club and player names through the original change menu.** Page through
  all clubs and players, keep the original limits and validation quirks, and see
  edited names throughout the game.
- **Reload the page and carry on where you left off, even mid-match.** Press
  Esc or tap Menu to open the game menu: resume, save to or load from five
  browser save slots, start a new game, export or import a save file, and set
  sound and display size. Play is frozen while the menu is open.
- **Save your game with keep and load it later.** Download a web save file,
  then continue playing or clear the game through the original question.
  Load the file using the control below the screen. Web saves are separate
  from original Spectrum tape files.
- **Continue into the next season.** Review the final table, earn the original
  league bonus and follow promotions or relegations. Player attributes and the
  schedule reset using the original rules, while your squad and finances carry on.
- **Buy players after your first match.** Bid in the original transfer market,
  retry refused offers at their increased asking value, or decline and return
  to management. Successful signings join as reserves.

### Changed

- **Highlights and timed screens take much less work to play.** The game no
  longer replays the rest of a sequence on every frame to find where it ends,
  and it crosses frames on which a keypress can change nothing in one step.
  Every result is unchanged: an automated 2,400-game baseline comes out
  identical and now runs in 2 minutes instead of 12.

### Fixed

- **Breaking.** **Highlights and timed screens follow measured original pacing.** Pitch drawing,
  players, outcomes and the cup celebration appear progressively; fresh and held
  keys shorten pauses according to the original keyboard behavior. New saves use
  version 3. Version-1 files and idle version-2 menu saves remain importable;
  active version-2 sessions cannot resume with the changed timing (#26).
- **Use the Spectrum's exact frame cadence when reseeding the game.** Commands
  now account for elapsed time at input dispatch even when animation callbacks
  are delayed, and opening the game menu freezes the clock without losing its
  fractional frame. Existing save files remain compatible (#25).
- **Game text uses the original eight-pixel glyph shapes.** Screens and match
  messages now render crisp bitmap text, including £, © and block graphics,
  with verified Spectrum colours, inverse and brightness combinations (#28).
- **Edited names reproduce Spectrum colours, controls and graphics.** Name output
  now preserves wrapping, inverse, brightness, flashing, graphics and the original
  PRINT error reports without losing the accepted name or game (#30).
- **Match highlights animate again.** Players and the ball move during
  highlights instead of freezing on the pitch until the final score appears.
- **Name-entry errors now scroll and retain earlier messages as in the original.**
  Long club names wrap fixtures, strength tables and season summaries correctly.
  Name-selection colours and the management menu's command column also match
  original emulator captures.
- **Pitch markings now match the original Spectrum pixels.** The curved marking
  uses the original eight rounded chords. Pixel checks also verify player and
  ball frames in both attack directions and both kit colours.
