**Modern mode's highlights react to the moment, and get to the point sooner.** After a save or a miss the shooter holds their head and the attackers jog back; after a goal the defenders hang their heads and the keeper fetches the ball from the net, where everyone used to stand still. A header lands from its leap instead of dropping to the ground, players no longer flick back and forth between facings, and build-ups reach their chance in under 15 seconds.
