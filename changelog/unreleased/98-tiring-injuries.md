**Under Enhanced rules, tired players get injured more often, and injuries last.** A player used to have the same 1-in-20 chance of injury before every match, and was back a week later. Now a player low on energy is up to three times as likely to be injured, can be out for up to three weeks, and doesn't recover energy while injured. The squad screen shows how many weeks each injured player has left. Every injury heals at the end of the season. Resting tired players and keeping a few spare ones now pays, and a club that never buys will often play short-handed. Original rules are unchanged, and games already started keep their rules (#98).
