**Match highlights in Modern mode have play, pause, rewind and next controls.** Tapping the pitch no longer skips a clip. Pause holds the match, and rewinding plays a clip again with the score as it stood then, all without changing how the game turns out. On a keyboard, Space pauses, ← rewinds and → or Enter moves to the next clip (#254).
