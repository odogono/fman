**No more sprinting in on a cross.** In the highlights, a header no longer streaks in at an impossible speed to meet a cross played to an empty patch of the box. The cross is now played from out wide, the winger waits a moment for the header to make their run, and the ball is floated or played shorter so they get to it at a believable pace. Players running onto any pass now get up to speed and hold it, rather than accelerating all the way to the ball.
