**In Modern mode, you can see every moment of the match while the clips play.** On a phone, tap the latest moment under the pitch to open a list of all of them, newest first. The clip keeps playing above it, and new moments join the list as they happen. On a wide screen the list sits in a panel beside the pitch, and after Skip the pitch shows full time with the list in the panel (#145).
