**Passes in Modern mode's highlights look like real passes.** A player now faces where they pass, throw, shoot or clear the ball. A pass keeps its pace instead of slowing down to wait for its receiver, a pass over 30 m goes in the air, and the final pass is played from within range of the shooter. Passes go forward through open space rather than through a defender, a pass back stays short, the ball runs ahead of whoever carries it, and a receiver no longer turns sharply as the ball is played.
