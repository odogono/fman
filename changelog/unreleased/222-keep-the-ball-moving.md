**Play no longer stops while a team-mate gets into place.** In Modern mode's match highlights, the player on the ball used to stand still for up to a second while a defender sprinted in and the shooter ran over. They now carry the ball on slowly, shielding it, and a defender close to the ball jockeys rather than sprinting in and stopping dead (#222).
