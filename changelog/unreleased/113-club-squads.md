**Under Enhanced rules, every club has a squad of its own.** All 64 clubs now field 12 invented players, and the players no club owns are free agents. Transfer offers come from the free agents, and a player you sell joins the club that bought him. At the end of each season clubs let go of their newest signings, and a fresh set of free agents comes up for transfer. Open **Clubs** in the game menu to see any club's squad by name and position. The squad screen tags goalkeepers with `G`. Matches play as before. Original rules are unchanged, and games already started keep their rules (#113).
