**Shooters no longer run half the pitch with the ball to shoot.** In Modern mode's match highlights, the shooter was often found 25 to 40 m out and then carried the ball a long way into range. Now midfielders push on in support, the pass before the final one goes forward, and the final pass waits for the shooter, so they're found in range, or shoot from range from where they are (#223).
