**Modern mode's weekly bills.** After each match, the bills now show wages, ground rent and loan interest with their total. The weekly balance is shown separately, signed and explained, so it no longer looks like the bills total. You also see how much was borrowed if your cash ran out, and your loan against the credit limit. Over the limit under the Enhanced rules, it says how far over you are and how many weeks you have left. A dismissal ends with a plain "Sacked" message and a Leave the club button. Classic mode keeps the original screens.
