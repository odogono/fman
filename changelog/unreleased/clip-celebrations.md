**Goals in Modern mode's highlights are celebrated, and keepers play on after a save.** The scorer throws their arms up and wheels away to the corner, the fans or their team-mates, and the nearest team-mates chase them down and celebrate around them, with the camera following. After a catch the keeper walks out with the ball as a team-mate breaks wide to take it; after a parry they get back to their line while an attacker goes to take the corner.
