**Modern mode picks the team on its own screen.** Before each match the squad is listed by Defence, Midfield and Attack, with the original's `p` and `i` markers and the two teams' strengths. Tap Change team, then tap reserves to add them. As in the original, a 12th pick means tapping one player to drop before you can carry on. Kick off and Done stay at the foot of the screen (#44).
