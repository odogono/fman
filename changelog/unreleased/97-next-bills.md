**Modern mode warns you before the bills catch you out.** While the main menu waits, the Home screen shows what next week's bills will cost. If your cash and the gate won't cover them, a warning says how much you'll borrow, whether it takes you over your credit limit (and how many weeks of grace you'd have under the Enhanced rules), or that it will get you sacked unless you find the money first. Before a cup round, whose draw is still hidden, it warns of the worst case (#97).
