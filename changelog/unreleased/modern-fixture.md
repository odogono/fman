**Modern mode's next fixture.** Before you pick your team, the fixture now shows both clubs side by side, with where each stands in the table (or its division, in the cup), whether you're at home or away, and for a cup round who you've been drawn against. The top bar now names the competition, "F.A. Cup – Round 1" rather than just "1", in both modes.
