**Modern mode shows the match on its own screen, and is now the default.** The original pitch plays out at full width under a scoreboard, with both teams' strengths below it. Hold the Hold to hurry button, or a finger on the pitch, to shorten the pauses between attacks, as a held key does. Under Enhanced rules the ticker lists each moment newest first, with Skip below it. Screen readers hear each goal or miss. New players start in Modern mode; if you already chose Classic, you keep it, and Presentation in the game menu's Options switches at any time (#45).
