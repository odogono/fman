**In Modern mode, each moment of an Enhanced match now plays as a short clip on a top-down pitch.** Some moves start from a free kick, a throw-in, a goal kick or a corner, and attackers stay onside. The camera follows the ball over part of the pitch, with a mini-map of the whole of it. It zooms in for the shot, and the shooter's name shows. The score and the ticker line change when the ball arrives, not before. A tap on the pitch ends a clip, and Skip shows every moment at once. Simple shapes stand in for the players for now. If your browser asks for less motion, you get the ticker as before (#144).
