**Defenders now clear chances in Enhanced matches.** Some moments are now clearances: a defender cuts out the final pass or blocks the shot, and clears it upfield or into touch. In Modern mode the clip shows it happen. Saves become rarer to make room, to match how the original's attacks end, and goals are just as likely as before. Near misses now read "Missed", since few hit the post. Games you've already started keep their rules (#150).
