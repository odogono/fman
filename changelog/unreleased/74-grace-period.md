**Under Enhanced rules, going over your credit limit gives you four weeks to clear it before you're sacked.** It used to end the game at once, most often straight after a relegation lowered the limit beneath your debt. The bills screen counts the weeks down; sell players, repay or win your way back under before they run out. Borrowing is still refused while you're over, and interest keeps mounting. Original rules are unchanged, and games already started keep their rules (#74).
