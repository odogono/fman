**Modern mode's new-game setup.** Starting a game now has its own screens: type your name, pick your club from each division's clubs in their colours (you'll start in Division 4, whichever you choose), and pick a level from the seven in their own colours. Changing your level from the menu marks the current one, and the question after a save has Keep playing and Stop buttons.
