**Throw-ins no longer go back towards the thrower's own goal.** In Modern mode's match highlights, a throw-in deep in a side's own half was sometimes thrown back towards its own box. That left the build-up too far to go, so a shooter from range ran a long way with the ball or the chance came late. Throw-ins now go up the line or across (#241).
