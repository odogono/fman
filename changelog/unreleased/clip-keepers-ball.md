**Keepers and the ball behave more like the real thing in Modern mode's highlights.** Keepers stand on the angle and come off their line further the further away the ball is. They sometimes come out to catch a cross before it can be headed, and after a catch they throw or roll the ball to a team-mate instead of always kicking it. Crosses and shots bend a little, long balls bounce before they're controlled, clearances run on along the grass, and a first touch knocks the ball on for the player to gather.
