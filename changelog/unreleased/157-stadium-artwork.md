**Modern mode's highlights are played in a stadium.** The goals have
posts, a crossbar and a net that players and the ball go in front of or
behind. Advertising boards ring the pitch, and stands of fans, mostly in
the home side's colours, rise beyond them. When a goal goes in, the
scorers' fans stand up and cheer. The ball is a little bigger too (#157).
