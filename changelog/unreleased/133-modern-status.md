**Modern mode shows your club's status on its own screen.** Print score etc now lists your managerial rating, level, seasons, cash, loan, morale, league position and league match in large, easy-to-read rows, with Done to go back to the menu. On a wide screen the same status sits beside the Home screen while the menu waits. Like Modern mode's other screens, including Pick team, it isn't copied by Print screen; Classic mode still copies the original screens (#133).
